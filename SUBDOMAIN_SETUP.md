# 🌐 How to Add a Custom Subdomain in Cloudflare Pages

Follow these simple steps to point your custom subdomain (e.g. `portfolio.skoriyatech.com` or `hasnain.ueeindia.com`) to your portfolio!

---

## Step 1: Open Your Cloudflare Pages Project
1. Log in to [Cloudflare Dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/).
2. Go to **Workers & Pages** -> Select your `hasnain-portfolio` project.

---
